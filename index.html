Featured Project

Food Desert Analysis & Solutions — GEOG 392

ArcGIS Pro Python Toolbox · Team project

A custom ArcGIS Pro toolbox that finds food deserts in any Texas county and ranks the best locations for new grocery stores. Enter a county name and it clips Census block groups and store locations, maps everything outside a 1.6 km store buffer, and scores candidate sites by population density and distance to the nearest store.
